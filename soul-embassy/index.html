He does not remember where he comes from. So he travels. Everywhere he lands, he stays awhile and lives amongst the people, learns their ways, writes it all down true — then moves on, still searching for home. Some places keep him longer, when there is more to learn: he climbs for the very top of every place he visits, because the top is where the knowledge, and the secrets, are kept. And he believes that at the very top, in the end, he will find the secrets to himself.
