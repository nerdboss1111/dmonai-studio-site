DMONAI YouTube Uploader

Privacy Policy

This page describes the DMONAI YouTube Uploader, a private tool operated by D'MONAI Studios.

Who uses the Uploader

The Uploader is used by D'MONAI Studios only, to upload and manage videos on its own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no external users or sign-up.

How it connects to YouTube

The Uploader connects to YouTube only with the channel owner's own Google consent, and only to publish and manage the owner's own videos.

Personal information

The Uploader does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information about any other person. It keeps no visitor data, runs no advertising, and uses no third-party trackers.
